Sample timetables for trying Profile -> Timetable -> Import.
Copy any of these to your phone, then choose it in the app.

timetable.xlsx                      Excel file (read by the server, no AI)
timetable-photo-days-down.png       a picture: days down the side, with merged double periods
timetable-photo-days-across.png     a picture: days across the top
timetable.pdf                       the same timetable as a PDF (a picture inside a PDF: read as a picture)
university-style-timetable.pdf      a PDF with real text, laid out like a university timetable
                                    (times split over two lines, merged cells, break columns, course list):
                                    read exactly, with no AI and no picture reading

The picture and PDF are read on your phone (free). All four should give the same classes:
Monday: Maths 09:00-09:50, Physics 09:50-10:40, CS Lab 10:50-12:30, DBMS 13:30-15:10 (and so on).
Delete the classes afterwards if you don't want them: use "Replace" when you import your real timetable.
